The app

Litestreak tracks habits you choose. You set your goals and decide how to work on them. The app records your check-ins and shows progress. Calorie and nutrient targets are estimates for healthy adults.

Litestreak is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet.

Purchases and subscriptions

When upgrades are available, Apple App Store or Google Play shows the price and the terms before you confirm. A one-time purchase does not renew. A subscription renews unless canceled. Manage or cancel it through Settings > Manage or cancel subscription. Uninstalling Litestreak does not cancel a subscription.

On Apple, a subscription (including a free trial) renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. On Google Play, cancel before the next renewal to stop future charges. Use the store account you bought with to restore purchases. An Apple purchase does not transfer to Google Play, or the other way around.

For refunds or withdrawal requests, start with the store that billed you. On Apple, you can request a refund through Apple Support. For withdrawal, open your Apple Account purchase history and choose Withdraw Transaction where available. On Google Play, use Google Play Help. If you are in the EEA, see its refund policy for apps and in-app purchases. You can also contact us if you need help. Store procedures do not take away any withdrawal, refund or other rights you have under mandatory law.

Your records

Your own habits, check-ins, notes and food entries are yours. You can export them from Settings. Keep any exported files somewhere you trust.

Food data

Open Food Facts supplies food search and barcode results under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0). Its data can be incomplete or inaccurate. Check the product label when accuracy matters. The license still applies to the food catalog data cached or exported by Litestreak.
