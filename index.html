Litestreak Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Litestreak records habits and goals you choose and shows your progress. The app has no Litestreak account or cloud sync. Your history is stored on your device. This policy explains when information leaves it.

Who is responsible

Thor Kenneth, an independent developer in Norway, is responsible for personal data we handle. You can contact us at tekeshine@gmail.com.

What the app stores

  • Habits, check-ins, notes, schedules, reminders, timers, streak freezes, rest days and Holidays.
  • Quit and cut-down records, including start dates, slips and uses, notes, limits and costs.
  • If you use nutrition tracking: age, sex, height, weight, activity level, weight goals, calorie and nutrient targets, saved foods and food logs.
  • Settings such as appearance, App Lock and currency. The app also keeps dates you used it for local prompts and the date you last shared a backup.

Consumer health data

Some habit and nutrition records can reveal health information. When you set up nutrition tracking, the app asks for your consent to save these records before it lets you start a food log. A backup you choose to restore can contain those records. You can delete the nutrition habit and its records at any time.

Litestreak is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any condition. Talk to a doctor or dietitian before changing your diet.

The app uses private storage provided by your device. It does not add its own database encryption. App Lock asks your device to verify you before opening the app. Litestreak does not receive your fingerprint or face data. App Lock does not protect copies in backup files, widgets or on watches.

Food lookups

When you search or scan a barcode, the app sends the search words or barcode to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org). The request identifies the app and the developer's contact email. Open Food Facts receives your IP address as part of the connection. The app does not attach your profile or food history to the request. We use the request data to provide the lookup you chose. Open Food Facts has its own privacy policy.

On Android, Google ML Kit reads barcodes on your device. For diagnostics and usage analytics, it collects your device manufacturer, model, operating system version and build, and available machine-learning hardware. It also collects the app package name and version, an identifier for the app installation, scanner performance such as processing time, scanner version and settings such as image format and resolution, input and output sizes, event types and error codes. Google says it encrypts this data in transit and does not share it with third parties. This is separate from the lookup sent to Open Food Facts. On iPhone, the app uses Apple's built-in barcode reader. Photo food recognition is not available in the store build.

Widgets, watches and reminders

Widgets and connected watches receive habit names, progress or streak counts, and check-in status. This lets them show progress and, where supported, record check-ins. They can keep a copy until they next sync. Notifications are scheduled on your device. Habit names and reminder text can appear on the lock screen. On Android you can allow more precise timing for habit reminders in the system's Alarms & reminders settings.

Purchases

When purchases are set up, RevenueCat helps the app load offers and check whether you have access to paid features. This can happen before you buy. RevenueCat and the store handle a generated app identifier where needed, purchase and subscription status, and receipt information. We use this to show available offers and carry out purchases, including restoring access you bought. Apple or Google handles payment. Litestreak does not receive your card details. Purchase records are kept under the providers' policies.

Error reports

Error reporting is off unless we enable Sentry in a build. When it is on, the app can send reports. We use them for our legitimate interest in finding and fixing app errors. A report can include the kind of error, where it happened in the code, the app version and details about your device and operating system. The app is set up without screenshots or screen layouts in reports. It removes error text, user fields, recent activity and request data from reports it filters. Sentry receives connection information such as your IP address. When reporting is enabled, Sentry stores error reports in Frankfurt, Germany. Sentry keeps error reports for 30 days on its Developer plan.

Feedback

If you send feedback, the app adds its version and your operating system version to your message. Without a screenshot, it opens an email draft. If you attach a screenshot, you choose an app or recipient from the share menu. Check what you send and avoid including private information. We have a legitimate interest in using feedback you send us to respond and improve Litestreak. We keep it as long as needed for those purposes and any legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data or use it for advertising.

Deleting, exporting and backing up

Deleting a habit removes its history. Deleting the nutrition habit also removes the profile, saved foods and food logs. Settings > Delete all data clears your habits, Holidays, nutrition records and other history from the app. It clears saved timers and cancels scheduled notifications. App Lock, appearance and purchase settings remain. Deleting from the app does not delete files you saved or shared, store records, or copies stored elsewhere. A disconnected watch can keep its last display until it syncs again.

Your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups may include app data. On Android, Litestreak is configured to exclude its data from automatic cloud backup and device transfer. Settings > Export data lets you share a CSV history and a JSON backup. These files are not encrypted by Litestreak. They can include private notes and nutrition records. Save or send them only where you want them. The JSON backup contains your tracking data. It does not carry over purchase access, App Lock or other device settings. Restoring it replaces the tracking data on the phone.

Your rights and contact

Depending on where you live, you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your personal data. You may also have the right to object to or limit how we use it. If we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it. To stop nutrition tracking and remove its local records, delete the nutrition habit or use Delete all data.

For information we hold or to make a privacy request, email tekeshine@gmail.com. We normally respond to GDPR requests within one month. If we need more time, we will let you know. In Norway, you can complain to Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.no). Elsewhere, you can contact your local data protection authority.

Children

Litestreak is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Nutrition targets are for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

We publish the current policy in the app and date each update. We will let you know about changes to how we use your data where required.
