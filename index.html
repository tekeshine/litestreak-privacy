Litestreak Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 16, 2026

Litestreak ("the app") is a habit, streak and nutrition tracker. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who receives it, and your rights.

Who Is Responsible

The app is published by its independent developer, Thor Kenneth (Norway), who is the data controller for any personal data we receive. Contact: tekeshine@gmail.com.

The app has no account system. Most data stays on your device, where you control it. Optional photo food recognition, available in configured test builds, uses a server as described below.

What Is Stored On Your Device

  • Habits and their settings: names, icons, colours, schedules, reminders and reminder messages you write, timers, notes you add, archive state.
  • Your history: daily check-ins, streak freezes, rest days and notes on individual days.
  • Nutrition data, if you use nutrition tracking: sex, age, height, weight, activity level, weight goal and rate, calculated calorie and macro targets, the foods you log (name, nutrients, amount, time) and food products you have looked up.
  • App settings, such as appearance and App Lock.

Body measurements, weight goals and food logs can reveal information about your health. We store them only so the app can calculate your targets and track your nutrition habit, and only after you agree to it in the nutrition set-up screen. They never leave your device except as described below.

This data is kept in the app's private storage on your device. It is protected by your device's own security (passcode, device encryption) and, if you turn it on, App Lock. It is not additionally encrypted by the app, so anyone who can unlock your device or extract its storage could read it.

Who Receives Data, And Why

  • Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a non-profit food database: when you search for a food or scan a barcode, the search text or barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. Your IP address and device information are visible to them as with any web request. No other app data is sent. Legal basis: providing the feature you asked for. See openfoodfacts.org for their own privacy policy.
  • Photo food recognition (configured test builds only): after you choose Continue, your selected photo is sent through our Cloudflare server to Anthropic to identify foods and estimate portions. Identified food names are sent to USDA FoodData Central for nutrition matches. These providers can receive network information such as IP addresses. The app does not add the photo to your food log; it saves only the food entries you confirm. Avoid photos containing people or private details; manual entry is available instead.
  • RevenueCat, Apple App Store and Google Play: if you buy or restore a purchase, these providers process an anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, and store receipts to manage your entitlement. We never receive your payment details. Legal basis: performing the purchase contract.
  • Email or share apps you choose: if you send feedback, the text you write, the app version, your device's operating system version and any screenshot you attach are sent through the email or share app you pick, to tekeshine@gmail.com or wherever you share them. We use feedback only to answer you and improve the app, and delete it when it is no longer needed for that.
  • Paired watches and widgets: habit names and today's status are shared with your own home-screen widget and paired Apple Watch, Wear OS or Garmin watch so they can show and record check-ins. This happens between your own devices (via Apple, Google or Garmin's connection services), not through us.
  • Reminders are scheduled by your device's own notification service. Reminder text you write appears on your lock screen.

We do not sell your data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking SDKs.

How Long Data Is Kept

Data on your device is kept until you delete it: deleting a habit removes its history; deleting your nutrition habit removes your nutrition profile, food products and food log; Settings > Delete all data erases everything. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from the device.

Backups: on iPhone, app data is included in your iCloud or computer backups (governed by Apple's terms) and can come back if you restore a backup. On Android, Litestreak's data is not included in device backups. Settings > Export data gives you a complete copy you can keep yourself.

Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat and the app stores under their own retention policies. Feedback emails are deleted when no longer needed to handle your request.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK), you have the right to access, correct, export and delete your personal data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent at any time (for nutrition data: delete the nutrition habit or use Delete all data), and to complain to a data protection authority. In Norway this is Datatilsynet (datatilsynet.no); elsewhere in the EEA, your local authority.

Because your app data lives only on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the app: edit or delete anything, export everything, or delete all data. For anything we hold (feedback emails) or questions about purchase data, email tekeshine@gmail.com. We reply within one month.

Children

Litestreak is not directed at children under 13, and nutrition targets are for adults (18+) only. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Not Medical Advice

Litestreak is a tracking tool. Calorie and macro targets are general estimates, not medical or dietary advice.

Changes To This Policy

We will show updated versions in the app with a new effective date. If a change affects how your data is used, we will point it out in the app before it applies.
