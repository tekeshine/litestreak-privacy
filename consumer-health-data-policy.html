Litestreak Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

Updated November 20, 2026. This policy applies to the version of Litestreak with the health data controls described here.

Litestreak is provided by Thor Kenneth, an independent developer in Norway. For a health data question or request, email tekeshine@gmail.com.

Health data we collect, where it comes from, and how we use it

  • Nutrition records. You enter details such as your age, sex, height, weight, activity level, goals, and the foods and amounts you log. Litestreak calculates calorie and nutrient targets on your device. When you choose an Open Food Facts result, the product and nutrition facts come from its catalog and can become part of your saved foods and food log.
  • Quit and cut-down records. You choose the habit and enter its start date, slips or uses, notes, limits and costs. Litestreak uses these entries to show goals, trends and progress. Habit names, reminders and notes can also reveal health information if you include it.
  • Consent and backup records. The app saves the version of the health data notice and when you agreed. A backup you choose to restore can contain the same nutrition and quit or cut-down data. If you have not agreed to the current notice, we ask before restoring those records.
  • Health information you choose to send. A support message, attachment or screenshot can include the health information you put into it. We use what is needed to answer your question or handle your request. Please remove private details we do not need.

We ask for your permission to use health records for nutrition and quit or cut-down tracking. If you already have these records and have not agreed to the current notice, we show a notice on Home so you can review your choice. You can still use ordinary habits without agreeing. Litestreak keeps your history on your device and has no Litestreak account or cloud sync.

Data that can leave the app and who receives it

  • Food catalog provider. When you request a food lookup, Open Food Facts receives the search words or barcode you submit and the IP address and app identification needed for the connection. Search words could reveal health information if you enter it. Use a product name or barcode. The app does not attach your body profile, food diary or quit history.
  • Device and backup services you use. Widgets, notifications and paired watches can display names or progress, including names that reveal a health habit. If you choose to export or share a backup, the app or recipient you choose receives the file. The files are not encrypted by Litestreak. Your iPhone's device backup may also contain app records, depending on your settings.
  • Support and email services. If you send health details to us by email or share a screenshot, we and the email or sharing providers involved in delivery can receive what you choose to send. We use it to respond. We may disclose information when required by law.

We have no affiliates that receive consumer health data. We do not sell consumer health data or share it for advertising.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to confirm whether we collect, share or sell your consumer health data, give you access to it, and provide a list and contact information for third parties and affiliates with whom we shared or sold it. You can withdraw permission for collection or sharing and ask us to delete your consumer health data.

In the app, use Settings to withdraw health data consent. If you confirm, Litestreak deletes your nutrition records and quit or cut-down habits and their histories from this device. You can export your own records from Settings. Manage files you exported and copies you shared through the device, app or service you used. A disconnected watch can keep its last display until it syncs or you clear it. Where we shared health data and the law requires it, we will notify the applicable recipients of your deletion request.

To make a request, email tekeshine@gmail.com and tell us the action you want. Please do not attach a health log or backup. We may ask for information needed to verify that the request is yours. We will respond without undue delay and within 45 days unless the law allows more time and we notify you. We can help you use your phone's controls for records stored there.

If we decline your request, you can reply to our decision or email the same address with the subject “Health data appeal.” We will explain the outcome within 45 days. If we deny the appeal, you can contact the Washington Attorney General.
